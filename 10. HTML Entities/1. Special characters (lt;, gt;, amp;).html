Special Characters Example

In HTML, certain characters have special meanings and cannot be used directly. Instead, they must be represented using character entities. Here are some examples:

  • &lt;: Represents the less-than sign (<).
  • &gt;: Represents the greater-than sign (>).
  • &amp;: Represents the ampersand (&).

To display these characters in HTML content:

  • Use &lt; for less-than sign: <
  • Use &gt; for greater-than sign: >
  • Use &amp; for ampersand: &

These character entities ensure that the special characters are displayed correctly in HTML, without being interpreted as part of the markup.
