Non-breaking Space Example

In HTML, the non-breaking space character ( ) is used to insert space between words or elements that prevents browsers from collapsing multiple consecutive spaces into a single space. Here are some examples:

  • Normal text: Hello World
  • Text with regular spaces: Hello World
  • Text with non-breaking spaces: Hello   World

Without using non-breaking spaces, multiple consecutive spaces will be collapsed into a single space when rendered in the browser. Non-breaking spaces ensure that the spaces are preserved as intended.
