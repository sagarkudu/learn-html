Underlining Text in HTML

This is an example demonstrating text underlining in HTML. You can use the <u> tag to underline text.

For instance, you might want to underline important terms or highlight specific words in your content.

Keep in mind that underlining is often used sparingly, as it can be associated with hyperlinks. In most cases, it's recommended to use other text formatting options like bold or italic for emphasis.
