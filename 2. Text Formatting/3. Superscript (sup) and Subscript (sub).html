Superscript and Subscript in HTML

This is an example demonstrating the use of superscript (<sup>) and subscript (<sub>) in HTML.

In a chemical formula, you might have H2O for water.

The mathematical expression a2 + b2 = c2 represents the Pythagorean theorem.

For footnotes or references, you can use numbers1.
