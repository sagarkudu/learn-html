  • The alt attribute in this <img> tag describes the content of the image.
  • Meaningful alternative text improves accessibility and helps users understand the context of the image.
  • The alt attribute provides alternative text for screen readers and is displayed if the image cannot be loaded.
  • It is important to use descriptive and meaningful text in the alt attribute to convey the content or purpose of the image.
  • Omitting the alt attribute may negatively impact accessibility and user experience.
